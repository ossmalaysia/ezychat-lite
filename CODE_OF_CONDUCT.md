# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. All contributors and maintainers are expected to follow it in every
project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel). The full text at
the link above is authoritative.

## Our pledge (summary)

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
maintainers privately by opening a private report through GitHub Security Advisories on this
repository, or by contacting the maintainers via GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant 2.1 Enforcement Guidelines (correction, warning,
temporary ban, permanent ban).
